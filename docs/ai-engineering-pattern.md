## 5 parts of AI Engineering Patterns

### 1. Models
- Understanding the foundational AI models, their capabilities, and how to effectively integrate them in to applications.

### 2. Prompting
- Mastering the art of crafting effective prompts to get reliable, consistent outputs from AI models.

### 3. Context(RAG)
- Implementing context in your applications (Retrieval Augmented Generation) to enhance AI repsonses with relevant external knowledge and data.

### 4. Orchestration (Agents)
- Building and managing AI agents that can coordinate multiple tasks and work together to solve complex problems

### 5. Evals & Observability
- Implementing robust evaluation frameworks and monitoring systems to measure and improve AI system performance.
